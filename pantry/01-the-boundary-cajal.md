**Source:** `chapters/01-the-boundary.md`
**Mode:** `/scan silent`
**Domain note:** AI+1 practitioner handbook / social media marketing. Chapter 1 establishes the foundational distinction — pattern-shaped vs judgment-shaped work — and the symmetric cost of mis-drawing the line (over-delegation: Sports Illustrated, CNET; under-delegation: the solo manager's 46 lost days per year). Three author-placed figure references appear in HTML comments.

## Density Recommendation

**3 figures, Foundational density.** Each of the three author-placed references earns its place: a two-axis quadrant that makes the symmetric failure visible, a Delegate vs Guard comparison that anchors the rest of the book, and a small "AI output → +1 decision node → publish" mechanism diagram. No further figures are needed; the chapter's other arguments (automation complacency, the "currently" caveat) are prose-shaped.

## Zone Map

- **MC:** The AI-output → +1-decision (accountability / relationship / context) → publish chain. Three structural inputs to the +1, one gate, one outflow.
- **VG:** The pattern-shaped vs judgment-shaped axis. Without a visual, readers collapse "AI used" with "AI used correctly" — the very confusion the chapter is trying to break.
- **PQ:** 46 lost days per year (one full working day per week of avoidable pattern-shaped hand-drafting). Not architected as a chart — the number is rhetorical, not a dataset.
