## Video Candidate Pass

**FIGURE 5.1 (Pearl's ladder):** STATIC SUFFICIENT. The conceptual hierarchy is read in one glance.
**FIGURE 5.2 (Eight-question checklist):** **MILD VIDEO CANDIDATE.** A sequential animation showing each checkpoint firing in order, with the bypass arrow as a visible shortcut, could dramatize the WYSIATI failure mode. Mild because the static funnel already carries the argument.
**FIGURE 5.3 (Two summaries):** STATIC SUFFICIENT. The divergence is structural, not dynamic.
**FIGURE 5.4 (Deming control chart):** **STRONG VIDEO CANDIDATE.** A time-series animation where the metric trace draws left-to-right, anomaly flags fire (some on common-cause, one on special-cause), and the practitioner's classify-moment intervenes, would make the Red-Bead trap visceral. The temporal nature of the argument is the point — you cannot tell common-cause from special-cause without watching the sequence.

**Video candidates identified: 1 strong + 1 mild.** Recommended: **Fig 5.4 (Deming control chart over time).** Animation makes the "stop reacting to every flag" lesson concrete in a way the static chart only suggests.
