**Priority: Critical.** This is the chapter's only quantitative-shaped figure and it carries an argument the prose cannot fully transmit: two metrics that *move in opposite directions* as AI assistance increases. The two lines cross near the center of the chart; mark the crossing point with a small Black filled circle. A small Sky Blue `#56B4E9` 15%-opacity vertical band sits to the right of the crossing point, indicating the zone where collective distinctiveness has dropped below individual quality — the structural homogenization zone. Data markers (small filled circles) every quartile on each line. Endpoint annotations placed post-typography. White background, flat vector, double-column 174mm.

## Figure 3.3 — Brief Specificity Anatomy

**Priority: Important.** The chapter argues that prompt specificity is the mechanism that converts a Delegate-safe task into a Delegate-failed task. The author note (line 29) requests a table; CAJAL converts it to a constraint-stack diagram so the *additive* nature of specificity is visible — each constraint narrowing the output space.

### Block 1 — Illustrae paste block

A vertical funnel-style composition. At the top, a wide Sky Blue `#56B4E9` filled trapezoid labeled (post-typography) as the unconstrained output space. Below it, six progressively narrower horizontal bands stack downward, each one Sky Blue at progressively higher opacity (15%, 25%, 35%, 45%, 55%, 65%) representing successive constraints — audience, format, length, angle, tone, explicit "do not." Each band narrower than the one above it, visualizing the narrowing of the output space. To the right of each band, a small Bluish Green `#009E73` filled circle marks the constraint *added* at that band. At the bottom, a narrow Bluish Green filled rectangle — the publishable-quality output zone. To the left of the funnel, a parallel Vermillion `#D55E00` filled vertical arrow runs top-to-bottom, with three small Vermillion filled circles marking what fails when each constraint is missing (annotations placed post-typography: "wrong register," "off-brand framing," "generic-fluent output"). White background, flat vector, single-column 89mm.
