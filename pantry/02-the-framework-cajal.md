## Figure 2.2 — Decomposition of a LinkedIn CEO Thread (Nine Steps)

**Priority: Critical.** The chapter's central operational example. The point is that most real tasks are *mixed* — six Delegate steps and three Guard steps in a single workflow. Without the visual, readers will continue to think in whole-task units. The author note (line 113) explicitly requests this diagram.

### Block 1 — Illustrae paste block

A vertical sequence composition, nine numbered steps stacked top to bottom. Each step is a filled rounded rectangle of consistent width. Color-coded: Bluish Green `#009E73` filled rectangles for Delegate steps, Vermillion `#D55E00` filled rectangles for Guard steps. The sequence from top to bottom: (1) Ideation — Vermillion; (2) Angle selection — Vermillion; (3) Outline drafting — Bluish Green; (4) First-draft prose — Bluish Green; (5) Voice pass — Vermillion; (6) Hook refinement — Vermillion; (7) Hashtag and tag generation — Bluish Green; (8) Publishing time and platform tuning — Bluish Green; (9) First-hour comment monitoring — Vermillion (with the small sub-note that *sorting* inside this step is Delegate and *responding* is Guard, indicated by a small Bluish-Green-to-Vermillion split within the rectangle). Between consecutive steps, a Black `#000000` 1pt downward arrow. To the right of the three Vermillion (Guard) steps — angle selection, voice pass, hook — small Reddish Purple `#CC79A7` callout brackets connect to tiny annotation markers indicating *why* each is Guard (requires knowing CEO, sustained proximity, voice most exposed) — annotations placed post-typography. Tally at the bottom: a small two-tile summary, Bluish Green tile marked "6" and Vermillion tile marked "3," numbers post-typography. White background, flat vector, single-column 89mm or double-column 174mm depending on layout; recommend 89mm.
